Pai Gow Poker Houseway Trainer

Pick the two cards that go in the Low Hand. The Joker completes a Straight/Flush/Straight Flush if possible; otherwise it is an Ace.

Houseway rules reference
  • Five Aces: pair of Aces low unless a pair of Kings can go low.
  • Four of a kind + trips: highest pair low; full house or quads high.
  • Four of a kind + pair: pair low, quads high.
  • Four of a kind: A–J always split; 10–7 quads high if a King+ can go low, else split; 6s and below never split (next two highest low).
  • Straight/Flush/SF + trips: highest pair low (Full House strategy).
  • Full house + pair: higher pair low. Full house: pair low, trips high.
  • Two Aces + Joker with straight/flush: straight/flush high; one Ace and the other card low.
  • Straight/Flush/SF: two pair → Two Pair strategy; one pair → highest two cards that leave the straight/flush high (Ace + K/Q/J/10 pair exception: Ace low, pair high, unless the Ace can go low and keep the straight/flush); no pair → two highest cards that leave it.
  • Two trips: pair from higher set low. Trips: Aces → Ace + next highest low; otherwise trips high, next two highest low.
  • Three pair: highest pair low. Two pair: J+ big pair split; 10–7 both high only with an Ace low; 6–3 both high only with Ace/King low.
  • One pair: pair high, two highest others low. No pair: 2nd and 3rd highest low.
