Pai Gow Poker Practice

Pick the two cards that go in the Low Hand. The Joker completes a Straight/Flush/Straight Flush if possible; otherwise it is an Ace.

Houseway rules reference
  • Five Aces: pair of Aces low unless a pair of Kings can go low.
  • Four of a kind + trips: highest pair low; full house or quads high.
  • Four of a kind + pair: pair low, quads high.
  • Four of a kind:
    • Aces–Jacks: always split.
    • 10s–7s: quads high if a King or better can go low; otherwise split.
    • 6s and below: never split — quads high, next two highest cards low.
  • Straight/Flush/SF + trips: highest pair low (Full House strategy).
  • Full house + pair: higher pair low.
  • Full house: pair low, trips high.
  • Two Aces + Joker with straight/flush: straight/flush high; one Ace and the other card low.
  • Straight/Flush/SF:
    • With No Pair: Place the two highest cards in the low hand that leave the complete straight, flush or straight flush in the high hand.
    • With One Pair: Place highest possible two cards (pair or no pair) in the low hand that will leave any complete straight, flush or straight flush in the high hand.
      • Exception: With an Ace and a pair of Kings, Queens, Jacks, or Tens, play the Ace in the low and the pair in the high hand, unless you can place the Ace in the low hand and keep the complete straight, flush or straight flush in the high hand.
    • With Two Pair: Play according to Two Pair strategy.
    • With Three of a Kind: Place pair in the low hand, complete straight, flush or straight flush in the high hand.
  • Two trips: pair from higher set low.
  • Three of a kind:
    • Aces: Ace + next highest card low.
    • Kings and below: trips high, next two highest cards low.
  • Three pair: highest pair low.
  • Two pair:
    • Big pair Jacks or better: split the pairs.
    • Big pair 10s–7s: both high only with an Ace low; otherwise split.
    • Big pair 6s–3s: both high only with an Ace or King low; otherwise split.
  • One pair: pair high, two highest others low.
  • No pair: 2nd and 3rd highest low.

Note 1: when more than one (straight/flush/straight flush) is possible, play whichever leaves the better low hand; if two splits leave an identical low hand, prefer the stronger high hand (straight flush > flush > straight).

Note 2 (Joker foul rule): if using the Joker to complete a straight/flush/straight flush will make the low hand outrank the high hand, strike out that possibility for the Joker (the Joker cannot play that card), and re-evaluate the hand from the top.

Winner Prediction: the player must beat both of the dealer's hands to win; one each is a push; copies (exact ties) go to the dealer. If the player's Low Hand outranks the High Hand, the player fouls and loses automatically.
